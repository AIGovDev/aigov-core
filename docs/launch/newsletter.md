# Technical newsletter pitch

**Subject:** A policy gate for AI evidence: AIGov Core trust boundaries

**Pitch:** AIGov Core is an AGPL-3.0 Rust audit runtime that accepts structured evidence, applies configured policy and returns an explicit VALID, INVALID or BLOCKED state. The project pairs a five-minute local walkthrough with an export verifier and documents a crucial limit: a valid hash chain establishes consistency of supplied records, not external truth, completeness, identity or legal compliance. Its current benchmark is a 20-scenario synthetic study with two false accepts, and the authors are preparing an independent security review.

**Editorial angle:** what an append-only evidence chain can and cannot establish; where policy gates fit beside telemetry and model-evaluation tools.
