# Product Hunt draft

**Name:** AIGov Core  
**Tagline:** Policy-gated evidence and audit exports for AI systems  
**Topics:** Developer Tools, Artificial Intelligence, Open Source; confirm current taxonomy.

**Description:** AIGov Core is an AGPL-3.0 Rust audit runtime with Python tooling. It records supplied evidence in tenant-scoped ledgers, applies configured policy, derives VALID / INVALID / BLOCKED, and exports records for review. It complements observability and experiment tooling; it does not prove the truth of inputs, external action occurrence, or legal compliance.

**Maker comment:** I built this to explore how governance controls can live in the engineering path: evidence enters a ledger, policy determines whether a run can proceed, and the export can be checked later. The repository documents a five-minute local quickstart; clean-install reproduction is pending. The benchmark is a small synthetic study and the hosted viewer must be linked only after public deployment and browser verification.

**Publication gate:** verify demo link, public launch readiness, visual assets and current Product Hunt rules. Do not imply official platform partnerships or claim adoption outcomes.
